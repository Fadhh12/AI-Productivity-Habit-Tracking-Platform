## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an **Anti-Burnout, AI-Native Ecosystem**. Built for modern knowledge workers, creators, and professionals seeking sustainable daily momentum, it fundamentally departs from aggressive, guilt-inducing productivity tropes. 

### Core Ethos
- **Forgiving Momentum:** Failure states do not evoke shame. Missed habits, pauses, and skipped days are rendered in soft, neutral rest states rather than punitive red alert banners or harsh crosses.
- **Electric Utility with Warm Calm:** Visual contrast is achieved through an architectural dark slate sidebar housing navigation and deep focus tools, opening into an expansive, warm porcelain canvas punctuated with vivid chartreuse/lime energy beacons and soft pastel categorizations.
- **Gentle Intelligence:** AI integrations carry a dedicated signature (`✨ AI`), functioning not as an opaque black box, but as an adaptable partner inviting inline user correction, reflection, and proactive rest suggestions.
- **Form Language:** Organic, ultra-rounded modules (20px–24px radii), floating pill containers, and hyper-legible geometric sans-serif typography create a tactile, comforting workspace that feels both futuristic and grounding.

## Colors

The palette balances deep architectural anchors with an energetic focal point and restorative pastels:

- **Primary (`#CCFF00` / `#D4F638`):** Electric Chartreuse/Lime serves as the singular beacon for active states, positive streaks, active navigational selections, and high-priority primary CTAs. It commands instant attention without causing alarm.
- **Secondary (`#1E2029` / `#16171D`):** Deep Obsidian Slate drives structural framing (such as fixed left-side navigation decks, dark mode cards, and elevated tooltips), grounding the interface with high-contrast authority.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` / `#EDE9FE`):** Soft Lavender provides the primary semantic hue for AI-driven digests, reflections, and generative planning prompts.
- **Neutral (`#18181B`):** Carbon Black provides crisp, maximum-legibility typography across canvas containers, paired with cool secondary grays (`#71717A`).
- **Semantic Rest Tones:** In accordance with non-punitive guidelines, missed checks or paused streaks strictly avoid red. Instead, they render in `state-skipped-neutral` (`#E4E4E7`) accompanied by reassuring microcopy. Categorical domains leverage gentle pastels (`accent-mint`, `accent-terracotta`, `accent-lavender`) paired with deep tone-matched ink labels.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric anatomy, clear structural terminals, and balanced open counters.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Displayed with confident weight (`700`), slight negative letter-spacing, and tight vertical rhythm to create commanding section milestones.
- **Section Headers (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Guide the eye across multi-card modular dashboards, maintaining rhythm with semi-bold weighting (`600`).
- **Body & Editorial (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Generous line heights ensure frictionless scanning during morning summaries and reflective evening reviews.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata (`label-md`, `label-sm`, `caption`):** Optimized for category tags, tolerance countdown indicators, and inline metadata badges. Always set with subtle letter tracking to maintain high clarity at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses an asymmetrical dual-canvas framework:

1. **Fixed Structural Dock:** An authoritative navigation rail (`260px` fixed desktop width) housing brand identity, route links, and mobile bridge modules. On mobile, this transitions into a bottom thumb-reach utility dock.
2. **Dynamic Modular Canvas:** An expansive fluid grid (`12 columns`, `1.5rem` gutters) on desktop, adjusting to `6 columns` on tablet and a single-column thumb stack on mobile devices.

### Anti-Burnout Density Constraints
- **Habit Clamping:** The primary daily execution dashboard strictly clamps habit cards to a maximum of 5 concurrent items to preserve cognitive focus and eliminate choice paralysis.
- **Internal Island Padding:** Cards maintain a generous internal breathing room (`space-lg`, `1.5rem`) preventing dense data crowding.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Permanent dark rail + 3-column modular masonry widget canvas.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Slim collapsed icon rail + 2-column widget layout.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Hidden sidebar, persistent sticky bottom bar within one-handed sweep range, edge-to-edge card carousel padding with `1rem` outer screen margin.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, skeuomorphic drops in favor of soft ambient diffuse shadows and high-contrast tonal containment:

- **Level 0 (App Canvas):** Flat backdrop base (`#F5F5F7`), warm off-white that shields user eyesight from pure `#FFFFFF` glare.
- **Level 1 (Content Cards & Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring low-contrast outlines (`1px solid #F0F0F2`) paired with an ultra-diffused atmospheric lift:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.02);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Modals):** Active dropdown menus, Quick-Add natural language processing bars, and calendar popovers carry a buoyant shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 23, 29, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 23, 29, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Tactile Navigation Anchor):** The dark slate navigation rail (`#16171D`) operates on an inverted elevation paradigm, utilizing interior depth with tone-on-tone elevated card docks (`#21232D`) and crisp dark borders (`1px solid #2A2D3A`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces an **ultra-rounded, tactile aesthetic** that evokes approachability and organic friendliness:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Standardized to a sweeping `20px` to `24px` corner radius (`rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl` equivalents), softening visual perimeters and establishing a welcoming rhythm.
- **Buttons, Badges & Pills:** Full continuous pill shapes (`9999px` border-radius) for quick actionable items, tab switchers, status labels, and search fields.
- **Icon Containers:** Squircle geometries (`14px` radius) serving as balanced backdrops for category and metric icons.
- **Prohibited Geometry:** Hard `0px` sharp corners and abrasive geometric angles are strictly prohibited across all customer-facing touchpoints.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid electric chartreuse (`#CCFF00`) with carbon black text (`#18181B`), full pill radius (`9999px`), `12px 24px` padding, font weight `600`. Hover state triggers a subtle brightness lift (`#D4F638`) and smooth 1.02x scale transformation.
- **Secondary Action:** Crisp white card container (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle border (`1px solid #E4E4E7`), text `#18181B`. Hover transitions background to `#F4F4F5`.
- **Navigation Item (Sidebar):** Pill shape. Inactive items feature muted gray text (`#A1A1AA`) on transparent backgrounds. Active item features solid lime background (`#CCFF00`), carbon black text (`#18181B`), and matching dark iconography.

### 2. Habit Cards & Status Indicators
- **Card Structure:** `20px` border-radius, pure white surface, `20px` padding. Contains category squircle icon, habit title, frequency label, and right-aligned completion trigger.
- **Success State:** Circular indicator fills with `#CCFF00` or `#18181B` with an animated smooth checkmark.
- **Neutral / Skipped State (Non-Punitive):** Soft gray fill (`#F4F4F5`) with subtle ring (`#E4E4E7`). Contains an unobtrusive horizontal dash or curved gentle curve—**strictly never a red cross or 'X'**.
- **Secondary Status Copy:** Reassuring microcopy displayed beneath missed habits (e.g., *"Rest day applied — streak preserved"* or *"Pause within tolerance"*).

### 3. AI Attribution Badge & Digest Cards
- **Badge (`✨ AI`):** Pill badge with soft lavender fill (`#EDE9FE`), purple label (`#6D28D9`), and sparkling star glyph.
- **Digest Container:** Subtle linear gradient transition (`from #FFFFFF to #FAF5FF`), wrapped in a delicate border (`#E9D5FF`). Includes an inline interactive trigger: *"Not quite accurate? Adjust"*.

### 4. Input Fields & Quick-Add NLP Bar
- **Natural Language Bar:** Floating capsule input (`9999px`), white surface with ambient elevation level 2, leading sparkle icon, placeholder text in `#71717A` (*"E.g., Read 20 pages every night at 9pm..."*), trailing electric lime submit arrow.
- **Form Inputs:** `14px` border radius, `#F4F4F5` fill, zero outline in idle state; transitions to `#FFFFFF` with `2px solid #18181B` ring upon focus.

### 5. Categorization Chips & Tags
- **Activity Chips:** Micro-pills (`rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`, text size `12px`).
  - *Focus & Learning:* Pale mint (`#D1FAE5`) with dark green ink (`#047857`).
  - *Mindfulness & Wellness:* Warm terracotta (`#FFEDD5`) with deep orange ink (`#C2410C`).
  - *Deep Work:* Soft lavender (`#EDE9FE`) with purple ink (`#6D28D9`).

### 6. Progress Bars & Analytics
- **Linear Progress:** `8px` height, `#F4F4F5` track fill, `9999px` radius. Active progress fills in electric lime (`#CCFF00`).
- **Activity Bar Charts:** Slender vertical bars (`8px` to `12px` width) with rounded top caps; inactive days render in deep slate (`#18181B`), while the active/selected day illuminates in vibrant chartreuse with an attached elevated tooltip.
